# Contributing

Thanks for improving the Cybersecurity GRC Playbook.

## Contribution principles

1. Explain why a control exists, who operates it, how it works, and what evidence proves it.
2. Prefer primary sources: standards bodies, regulators, government publications, and professional assurance organizations.
3. Distinguish framework requirements, implementation guidance, legal obligations, contracts, and recommended practices.
4. Use fictional examples. Never commit confidential evidence, credentials, customer data, internal audit reports, or vendor questionnaire responses.
5. Summarize protected standards in your own words and link to official publications; do not reproduce lengthy copyrighted material.
6. Label illustrative risk scores, response targets, thresholds, and RACI assignments as examples to tailor.

## Document review checklist

- [ ] Plain-language definition and business purpose
- [ ] Scope, assumptions, and version/date context
- [ ] Key clauses, domains, criteria, or components
- [ ] Realistic enterprise workflow and team hand-offs
- [ ] RACI or cross-functional collaboration where relevant
- [ ] Sample artifacts or evidence requests
- [ ] Pitfalls and audit failure scenarios
- [ ] Interview questions with scenario-based answers
- [ ] Authoritative references
- [ ] Clear distinction between illustrative examples and mandatory requirements

## Pull requests

Use a descriptive title. Note the problem solved, sources checked, assumptions, and version-sensitive content that may need future review.
