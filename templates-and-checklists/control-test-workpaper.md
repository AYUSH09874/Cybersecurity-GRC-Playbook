## 3. Test plan

1. **Understand the control:** Walk through the process and confirm scope with the owner.
2. **Identify the population:** Document source system, report, date range, filters, row count, and completeness check.
3. **Determine sample:** Use the approved audit/test methodology. Document selection method, rationale, sample size, and any auditor-prescribed requirements. Do not invent a sample size to fit the evidence received.
4. **Execute procedures:** Inspect records, reperform calculations where appropriate, observe configuration, or trace activity from initiation through approval and completion.
5. **Record exceptions:** Capture the criteria, condition, exact sample, evidence ID, and impact. Clarify factual errors with the owner without changing the independent conclusion to meet expectations.
6. **Conclude and review:** Apply the methodology's rating definitions; document reviewer approval and follow-up actions.
