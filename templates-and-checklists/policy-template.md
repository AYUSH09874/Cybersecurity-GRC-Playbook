## 1. Purpose

Explain the business outcome, risk, obligation, or control objective this policy addresses.

## 2. Scope

Identify covered entities, employees, contractors, systems, information, locations, and relevant third parties. Explicitly document exclusions and the authority that approves them.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Executive sponsor | Approves policy and resolves material resourcing/priority decisions. |
| Policy owner | Maintains content, coordinates review, and monitors compliance. |
| Control / system owners | Implement requirements and maintain operational evidence. |
| Workforce / contractors | Understand and follow applicable requirements. |
| GRC / compliance | Maps requirements, tracks exceptions, and reports issues; does not automatically own every control. |

## 5. Exceptions

Define who may request, assess, approve, record, time-limit, and review exceptions. Require a documented business rationale, risk assessment, compensating controls, accountable risk acceptance, expiration date, and renewal process. Do not allow the requestor to self-approve unless formally authorized by policy.

## 6. Compliance, monitoring, and violations

Describe measurement, evidence, periodic review, escalation, and how suspected violations are handled under applicable HR/legal processes. Avoid promising specific disciplinary outcomes without authorized review.
